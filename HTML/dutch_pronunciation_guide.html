Golden Rules for Dutch Pronunciation

Open vs Closed Syllables

OPEN
Ends in vowel
ma-ken
Long vowel!
vs
CLOSED
Ends in consonant
man
Short vowel!
📏 Rule 1: Long Vowel in Closed Syllable
When a long vowel appears in a closed syllable, write it DOUBLE:
taal (ताल) = language, been (बीन) = leg
📏 Rule 2: Long Vowel in Open Syllable
When aa, ee, oo, uu appear in open syllable, write them SINGLE:
ha-ren (हारन) = hairs (NOT haaren)
📏 Rule 3: IE, OE, EU Never Change
These vowels stay the same in both open and closed syllables:
deu-ren (दुरन) = doors, die-ren (दीरन) = animals
📏 Rule 4: Final Letters Change
• Final D → T sound
• Final B → P sound
• Final -IG → -अख़ sound
📏 Rule 5: Stress Pattern
Stress is usually on the FIRST syllable:
WIN-ter, VA-der, LO-pen

Exception: Words with prefixes be-, ge-, her-, ont-, ter-, ver-, wan-
Stress on SECOND syllable: be-GIN, ver-RAAD

Double Letter Decision Tree

Is the syllable CLOSED (ends in consonant)?

↳ YES → Use DOUBLE vowel (aa, ee, oo, uu)

Example: taal, been

↳ NO (Open syllable) → Use SINGLE vowel (a, e, o, u)

Example: ma-ken, bo-men


Exception: ie, oe, eu NEVER change!

🧠 Memory Tricks & Mnemonics

🎯 The "G" Sound Trick
Think of clearing your throat or the Scottish "loch" - it's that scratchy sound!
Practice: goed (good), dag (day), licht (light)
🎯 EI = IJ Memory Hook
"Either way, it's the SAME!" - EI and IJ are pronounced identically.
trein = train, hij = he (both sound like "ay")
🎯 The UI House Trick
UI makes "ow" sound like you stubbed your toe in your huis (house): "OW!"
huis, tuin, muis all have that "OW" sound
🎯 OE = Moon Moo
OE sounds like "oo" in "moon" - remember the cow (koe) goes "moo" at the moon!
🎯 Final D/B Devoicing
"Dead letters lose their voice" - Final D→T, Final B→P
bed sounds like "bet", heb sounds like "hep"
🎯 The AA/EE/OO/UU Dance
In CLOSED syllables (trapped by consonants), long vowels need a partner - write DOUBLE!
In OPEN syllables (free to breathe), they dance alone - write SINGLE!
🎯 The "SCH" Sneeze
SCH = Say "S" then clear your throat "CH" = like sneezing!
schaap (sheep), school (school)
🎯 IE Never Changes Club
IE, OE, EU are stubborn - they NEVER double or change in open syllables!
deu-ren (NOT deuuren), die-ren (NOT dieren)
Quick Reference Card
Same sound, different spelling:
• EI = IJ (both "ay")
• OU = AU (both "ow")

Unique Dutch sounds:
• G/CH = throat-clearing "kh"
• UI = "ow" in "now"
• EU = rounded "uh"
• OE = "oo" in "moon"

Quick tips:
• R is always rolled
• J = English Y
• W = soft V
• Final D/B lose voice (→T/P)
